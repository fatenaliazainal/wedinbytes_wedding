---
name: Catalog preview style
description: Visual treatment for the public wedding-card catalog previews.
---

The public catalog and homepage card showcases use direct portrait invitation previews without a phone frame or outer white border; the catalog metadata and CTA sit inside the same compact card, while the All-in-One marketing section intentionally keeps a larger live phone preview to explain the product.

**Why:** The public card showcases should resemble physical invitation cards, while the All-in-One hero needs a phone visual to communicate the digital invitation product.

**How to apply:** Keep catalog/home card-grid previews portrait with a transparent background and subtle shadow. For the catalog, group preview, design identity, category badge, and order CTA into one compact card. Borrow structure from references, not their palette or styling; keep the WedInBytes rose/gray visual system. Keep the All-in-One phone preview, three concise feature columns, dual CTAs, and trust strip unchanged.
