---
name: Announcement email preferences
description: Consent and recipient-selection rule for Wedinstudio announcement emails.
---

Announcement emails include a one-click unsubscribe link beneath the email footer. A recipient who opts out must be excluded from every later announcement blast, even when an administrator manually selects recipients.

**Why:** Recipient selection is useful for targeted announcements, but it must not override a user’s email preference.

**How to apply:** Keep transactional notifications separate from announcements; show opted-out accounts as unavailable in admin recipient selection and enforce the opt-out again on the server immediately before sending.
