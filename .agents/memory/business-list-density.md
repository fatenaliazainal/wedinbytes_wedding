---
name: Business list density
description: Business order-form submissions and invitations should use compact table-like rows for larger lists.
---

Business Account lists should use compact table-like rows rather than large cards, so many order form submissions and invitations remain scannable in one view.

**Why:** Large cards become inefficient when a business has many invitations.

**How to apply:** Keep rows compact on desktop with clear columns and actions; stack the same information responsively on mobile without horizontal scrolling.
