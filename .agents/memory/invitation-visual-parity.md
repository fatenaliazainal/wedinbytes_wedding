---
name: Invitation visual parity
description: Buyer and Business Account invitations share the same public invitation presentation.
---

Business Account ownership and management may differ internally, but the public invitation should not add a business-branded closing section or otherwise visually distinguish itself from a Buyer invitation.

**Why:** The invitation's original closing layout is the product experience for every customer; business metadata belongs to account-management flows rather than the guest-facing invitation.

**How to apply:** Keep the shared invitation component's final sections identical for Buyer and Business Account invitations unless the user explicitly requests a guest-facing business attribution.
