==================================================
FORM FUNCTIONALITY
==================================================

The form should:

- Require the customer's name.
- Require a rating from 1 to 5 stars.
- Require a review message.
- Wedding date should be optional.
- Validate required fields.
- Show a loading state while submitting.
- Show a success message after submission.

Success message:
Thank you for sharing your experience with us! Your review has been submitted for approval.

==================================================
REVIEW MODERATION
==================================================

IMPORTANT:

New reviews submitted through the form should NOT appear publicly immediately.

Create a review status system:

- Pending
- Approved
- Rejected

Only APPROVED reviews should appear publicly on the Reviews page.

New reviews should automatically have the status:

Pending

The admin should be able to:
- View pending reviews.
- Approve reviews.
- Reject reviews.
- Delete reviews.
