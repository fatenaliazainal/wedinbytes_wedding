==================================================
NEW BUSINESS MODEL
==================================================

WedInBytes has TWO different customer types.

1. Buyer
2. Business Account

Business Account is NOT a collaborator.

Business Account is NOT assigned by Buyer.

Business Account does NOT edit Buyer invitations.

Business Account is NOT linked to Buyer invitations.

Please remove that architecture.

==================================================
BUSINESS ACCOUNT PURPOSE
==================================================

Business Account is another paying customer of WedInBytes.

They purchase a different subscription/package than Buyer.

They use WedInBytes to create wedding invitations for THEIR OWN CLIENTS.

They are NOT invited by Buyers.

They are NOT collaborators.

==================================================
INVITATION OWNERSHIP
==================================================

Business Account owns the invitations it creates.

Buyer owns the invitations they create.

There is never shared ownership.

There is never invitation collaboration.

Every invitation has exactly one owner.
