Improve the Reviews section for better scalability and user experience.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT modify any backend logic.
- Do NOT change the review submission process.
- Do NOT change the review database structure.
- Do NOT change APIs.
- Preserve all existing functionality.
- This is a UI/UX improvement only.

OBJECTIVE

The Reviews section must be able to handle hundreds or even thousands of reviews without requiring users to endlessly scroll.

Requirements

1. Reduce the size of every review card by approximately 20–30%.
   - Reduce card width proportionally.
   - Reduce internal padding.
   - Reduce font sizes slightly.
   - Reduce spacing between elements.
   - Keep readability high.

2. Keep the existing design language.
   - Same colours
   - Same rounded corners
   - Same shadows
   - Same typography style
   - Same star rating design

3. Add pagination below the review grid.

Example:
< Previous  1  2  3  4  5  ...  18  Next >

4. Show only a limited number of reviews per page.
   - Desktop: 6 reviews (3 columns × 2 rows)
   - Tablet: 4 reviews
   - Mobile: 4 reviews (2 per row or 1 per row depending on screen size)

5. Display review statistics above the review grid.

Example:

★★★★☆ 4.8
Based on 126 reviews

6. Add sorting options.

Sort By:
- Newest First
- Oldest First
- Highest Rating
- Lowest Rating

7. Add rating filters.

Filter:
- All Reviews
- 5 Stars
- 4 Stars
- 3 Stars
- 2 Stars
- 1 Star

8. Display review count below pagination.

Example:
Showing 1–6 of 126 reviews

9. Ensure pagination updates correctly after filtering or sorting.

10. Make the entire section fully responsive.

Desktop:
- 3 cards per row

Tablet:
- 2 cards per row

Mobile:
- 1 card per row

11. Keep card heights consistent.
If a review is too long:
- Limit preview to 3 lines.
- Show "Read More".
- Expand only that card when clicked.
- Do not affect the layout of other cards.

12. Smooth interactions.
- Fade transition when changing pages.
- Smooth hover effect.
- Smooth expansion for "Read More".

13. Performance.
- Do not render all reviews at once.
- Only render the reviews for the current page.
- Optimise for hundreds or thousands of reviews.

Design Goal

The Reviews section should feel modern, premium and scalable, similar to professional SaaS websites.

It should remain clean and fast even if there are 1,000+ reviews.

IMPORTANT

- Do NOT redesign the entire section.
- Do NOT change existing review functionality.
- Only improve the layout, pagination, filtering, sorting and overall user experience.
