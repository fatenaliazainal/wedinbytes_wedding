Redesign ONLY the visual appearance of the Wishes section into a modern "Letter Style" guestbook.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT modify any functionality.
- Do NOT change the carousel behavior.
- Do NOT change APIs, database, business logic, or existing animations.
- Only redesign the visual appearance of the wish cards.

Design Goal:
The wishes should feel like handwritten messages inside an elegant wedding guestbook instead of social media comments.

Requirements:

1. Remove the traditional card appearance.
   - No heavy box.
   - No thick borders.
   - No large shadows.
   - No social media comment styling.

2. Replace it with an elegant paper/letter aesthetic.
   - Warm ivory background (#FFFCF8 or similar)
   - Very subtle paper texture or paper-like gradient (CSS only, no image texture)
   - Rounded corners around 12–16px
   - Very soft shadow
   - Plenty of white space

3. Remove the avatar/initials completely.
   - Do not display any circular avatar.
   - Do not display initials like ZA, EZ, etc.

4. Layout:
   - Center the message.
   - Display the wish message first.
   - Display the sender's name underneath.
   - Display timestamp below the name if available.

Example:

────────────────────────

Semoga Allah memberkati
perkahwinan kalian dan
dikurniakan kebahagiaan
hingga ke syurga.

— Zaidi
2 hours ago

────────────────────────

5. Typography:
   - Message:
     - Slightly larger font
     - Comfortable line height
     - Elegant serif font if already available, otherwise keep the existing font.
   - Sender name:
     - Semi Bold
     - Slightly darker than timestamp
   - Timestamp:
     - Small
     - Muted color

6. Decorative elements:
   Add a very subtle floral divider or elegant ornament above and below the message.

Example:

❦

(message)

❦

Keep it minimal and elegant.

7. Animations:
   - Fade in
   - Slight upward motion
   - Smooth transition
   - No bouncing effects

8. Responsive:
   - Mobile
   - Tablet
   - Desktop

9. Keep the existing carousel, pagination dots, swipe gestures, and auto-slide exactly as they are.

10. The overall appearance should resemble a premium wedding guestbook or handwritten letter, not a social media comment section.

IMPORTANT:
